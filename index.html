Lisa: Hi, Ronald. I was reading an amazing article about future jobs and Artificial Intelligence. Do you think AI will replace teachers in our classrooms?
Ronald: Hi, Lisa. No, human teachers won't vanish; they will use AI tools to teach better.
Lisa: The article adressed something about medicine. Will doctors lose their jobs when robots perform surgeries?
Ronald: That's interesting. Advanced robots will assist doctors, but they won't make medical decisions alone.
Lisa: What about engineers? Will they become unnecessary if computers design everything automatically?
Ronald: Engineers will focus on bigger creative problems while software will handle repetitive designs.
Lisa: For example, my dad works as a mechanic, and I worry he might lose his job. Will mechanics still have employment opportunities once all cars are fully electric and automated?
Ronald: Smart mechanics will adapt quickly and will repair high-tech components.
Lisa: I like that. It will encourage people to perfect their skills. Experienced workers are in trouble, as traditional jobs won’t survive without major changes, and that terrifies me!
Ronald: Technology will transform these roles, but it will create exciting new opportunities.
Lisa: Will people feel secure about their future careers?
Ronald: Continuous learning will guide us, and humanity will thrive alongside new technology!
Lisa: I hope Artificial Intelligence won't make human skills completely obsolete!
Ronald: Don't worry, Lisa. Human creativity and empathy will always remain essential.
1. What is the main idea of the conversation?
2. In the sentence, "Experienced workers are in trouble, as traditional jobs won’t survive without major changes, and that terrifies me!", what does the word "terrifies" mean?
3. What does the expression "won't vanish" mean when Ronald says, "human teachers won't vanish; they will use AI tools to teach better"?
4. According to Ronald, how will advanced robots interact with doctors in the medical field?
5. How will engineers adapt to automated computer designs?
6. What can be inferred about Ronald's perspective on the future of human workers?
7. Which of the following statements is FALSE according to the dialogue?
